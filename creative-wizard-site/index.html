03 — The manufacturability read

Know what the design demands — before you sample.

  • Shade separation. Every palette is measured pair-by-pair against export lab-dip tolerance, so shades that could swap places between dye lots are flagged before they cost you a sampling round.
  • Colour stations. Screens, blocks, needles and weft colours are counted against the technique's real ceilings — and a better route is suggested when the design wants more than the machine gives.
  • Repeat fit. The declared repeat is checked against the product's true dimensions — full repeats centred, cut-offs called out in centimetres, with exact-fit suggestions. Rotary repeats are checked against standard screen circumferences; jacquard repeats against hook counts at your EPI.
  • Technique fit. Curated verdicts for every machine-on-product combination — and the studio learns your own verdicts from what you like, dislike and note.

04 — From design to production file

True repeats, born seamless.

Repeating designs are generated as one continuous cloth — the join is measured, and the design is kept only when the seam reads as cloth. When you're ready, the production pack delivers the files your printer actually uses:

True-size TIFF

Your tile at real dimensions with the DPI written in the file header — ready for the print house.

Tiled proof

A 3×3 preview with real straight, half-drop, brick or mirror layout — the honest proof of how the tile joins.

Colourway card

Every shade with its name, code and values — in RGB for screen or CMYK for print, the way your dyer reads it.

One zip

Everything in a single download. Cutting-pattern sheets unfold saved product shapes into sewable panels in centimetres.
